SECURITY

✅ Your data is safe:
   - Official Robinhood OAuth (you control)
   - Tokens encrypted locally (AES-256)
   - Never stored in code
   - Auto-refresh before expiry
   - Revocable anytime

To revoke access:
  rm ~/.robinhood_mcp_tokens.json
  OR go to: https://account.robinhood.com/settings/connected-apps
